A tiny circuit playground.

Place parts, connect outputs to inputs, and watch the signals change.

Try the half adder

  1. Move to the upper switch and press ENTER to toggle it.
  2. The upper light is the sum. The lower light is the carry.
  3. Move down twice to the second switch. With both switches on, sum is 0 and carry is 1.

More room to build

Choose 6×4, 9×6, or 12×8. Arrows scroll when you reach the edge. Expanding preserves your circuit.

Wires run in the gaps between parts; they never occupy a cell. You can place parts between connected gates.

Open the menu for an empty board. ENTER on an empty cell opens parts. To wire: choose the source, press W, move to the target, choose A/B with P, then ENTER.

A part can feed many inputs. Feedback loops are rejected. Save from the menu; undo remembers 16 changes.

The preview runs the same C simulation, UI and pixel drawing as the calculator build. Keypad hardware, archive storage and CE performance still require a calculator test.
